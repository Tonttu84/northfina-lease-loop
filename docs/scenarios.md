# Scenario cards: the full status

The README's [Status](../README.md#status) table is the short view: one row per scenario card of the brief ([CHALLENGE.md](../CHALLENGE.md) section 4) with the numbers the judges compare and the main test file. This page holds the rest: how the cards are tested, the full table with every test that proves a card, the assumption card 8 rests on, the pack's traps, the surprise-scenario drills, the one finding the cards turned up, the smart problems and the money-saved estimate. Amounts are in integer euro cents unless a € sign says otherwise.

## How the cards are tested

Every card runs twice: as a table-driven test on the real data pack in [`src/domain/scenarios/`](../src/domain/scenarios/) (pure, `npx vitest run src/domain/scenarios`), and end to end in [`src/server/scenarios/`](../src/server/scenarios/) on a real migrated database through the services the routes call: quote, the question flow, the decision, signing, payments, the exit, the passport and the audit chain (`npx vitest run src/server/scenarios`). No todo is left (`grep -rn "it.todo" src` is empty). The demo page runs each card from its own Run button through the same services (`src/server/demo-cards.ts`), and `src/server/demo-cards.test.ts` pins that every run leaves the card's numbers and lands on the page the judges compare. How the tests stay independent: [features/test-isolation.md](features/test-isolation.md). The pitch run is also clicked through in a real browser: `npm run test:e2e` builds the app and walks the demo page, the actor picker, the second-life page, the signing link, the language switch, the audit page and the catalogue with Playwright ([features/e2e-tests.md](features/e2e-tests.md)).

## Assumption (D31)

The pack has no first-lease date, so an item on its first life is aged on the residual curve from the day its lease started, as if every pack item were new on the shelf; an item with earlier lives is aged from 1 January of its model year (D11). This is how we treat it even where it may not hold for the real item; card 8's market value of 311 200 rests on it, and the other reading (202 523 as good) is pinned beside it in `src/domain/residual/market-value.test.ts`.

## Traps

`src/domain/scenarios/traps.test.ts`: the inclusive band edge (`laptop-002` at 199 999 is A, `laptop-003` at 200 000 is B; on v1 only, v2 prices both at 184 000); `ebike-010` below the table on v1 (inside band A on v2) and `farm-006` above it, each a code; the broken business ID; both twin pairs told apart by the matcher (`wait-02` gets the fair `ebike-007`, never `wait-13`; `wait-06` beats `wait-14` on D13's last step, the lower id); invoices on 31 Dec 2026 at 25.5 % and on 1 Jan 2027 at 26 %; 29 Feb 2028 due dates; the price list in force on 5 and 6 Nov. The reserved `ebike-009` is refused where its status is read: `src/server/quotes.test.ts` "refuses a reserved asset (ebike-009) with its status". Every customer × one asset per category decides with a known code: `src/domain/scenarios/customers.test.ts`.

## Surprise-scenario drills

`src/domain/scenarios/drills.test.ts` and `src/server/scenarios/drills.test.ts`: each changes the pack the way a judge's surprise could and passes it in. A price list v3 after 6 Nov moves `laptop-001` up to band B and `ebike-003` down to A from its first day; other band edges in the coefficient table move an item without a code change; a day no list covers is `no_price_list_on_date`. A fictional 27.5 % VAT from 1 Jul 2027 lands on the July invoice of a running contract and nowhere before it, the net unchanged. A 48-month laptop and, with a lower cap in the data, a 36-month one are refused with `term_over_category_max`. A lease from 29 Feb 2028 for 12 or 24 months is due on the 29th, on the 28th in February 2029, with no day billed twice or skipped. A returned laptop is wiped and its battery replaced at 500 cycles and goes to the laptop entry that fits (`wait-08`). A waiting list where nobody fits names each entry's failing step and relists the item at good. A company applies for an e-bike, a laptop and a farm machine with the decision its record gives (approved, signatory, PEP review where the PEP question is asked, broken business ID). Every asset status but `available` is refused at the quote with its status.

## Finding, fixed

The quote’s totals used to add the down payment and the buyout net while invoice 0 charges VAT on the down payment (card 7: 510 000 short). The pricing slice fixed it on `main` (`4ec3b2e`); `src/domain/scenarios/quote-schedule.property.test.ts` now proves that the quote total equals what the invoices add up to whenever one VAT rate covers the term, and "card 7: invoice 0 is the quote down payment with its VAT".

## Estimate (D80)

For lives before our records we have no record of the exact refurbishment costs, so we count only the costs we know were there, the steps the cost table does on every relisting (e-bike safety inspection 45 €, laptop data wipe 25 €, farm machine safety inspection 400 €), and the pages call that money saved estimated.
